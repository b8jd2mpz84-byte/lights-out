Lights Out

Formula start, stripped to the reflex. Darkness is the signal.

Rules and feel +
  • Five pairs light left to right. When they all go dark, that is the start.
  • Tap the gantry, or press Space. Esc stands down without logging a time.
  • Tap while any red is still on and it is a jump start.
  • Qualifying is five attempts. The quickest clean start is pole.
  • Sprint is ten. Each jump adds 0.500s to the total.
  • Season is eight rounds of three starts against the same five drivers. Lowest total time wins the round, a jump adds 0.6 s, and points build a championship table. Win the Club title to unlock the Pro field.
  • Race is five starts against five AI drivers. Points for P1 to P6 are 25, 18, 15, 12, 10 and 8. A jump scores nothing.
  • The clock starts the frame the lights go dark. Records stay on this device.
  • Under 100 ms is not a reaction. It counts as a jump, same as the real thing.
  • Times include your screen and touch latency, so compare scores on the same device.
  • While the lights run, the whole screen is the trigger.

Install it: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Full screen, works offline, and your records stop expiring.
